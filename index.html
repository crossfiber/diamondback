Before You Call

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. David comes out, finds where the animals are getting in and gives you a removal plan for your property before any work starts.

Do you use poison?

No. Rats and other wildlife are handled with traps and exclusion, never poisons or toxins.

Can you come at night or on a weekend?

Yes. Diamondback Wildlife is open 24 hours with emergency service every day of the week.

What happens after the animals are gone?

Entry points get sealed and the attic is cleaned and sanitized if it needs it. David follows up until nothing is getting back in.

Where do you work?

Port Charlotte and nearby areas along the Gulf Coast, from Sarasota down to Fort Myers.

Is the work guaranteed?

Warranties are offered. David goes over what's covered for your job at the inspection.
